About

Graphy is a calculator website inspired by Desmos. It features a graphing calculator that allows users to visualise mathematical equations by plotting them on a coordinate plane. The website also includes a scientific calculator inspired by the Casio fx-9910CW, a physical scientific calculator used in schools. It supports a wide range of mathematical operations including trigonometric functions, logarithims, roots and more. Graphy aims to bring the functions of a physical scientific and graphing calculator come to life in one accessible online platform. Its goal is to help users with ease of access, allowing them to understand mathematical concepts visually
